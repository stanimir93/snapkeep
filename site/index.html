What it does

Copied and saved

Paste right after you capture. Every screenshot also lands in ~/Pictures/Screenshots.

Previews that stay

Each capture opens a floating window that never closes on a timer. Keep several open at once.

Select text in the image

Live Text is ready as soon as the preview opens. Copy part of the text, or all of it.

Text-only capture

One shortcut copies the text in an area and skips the preview and the file.

Screen recording

Record an area or a window, then save it as an MP4, a small GIF, or both.

Quick drawing

Pen, highlighter, line, arrow, rectangle and text in four thicknesses. Grab a drawing to move, resize or delete it.

Snapping selection

The screen freezes while you select, and edges snap to windows and to the lines between panes.

Lightweight

Native Swift, no dependencies, no network access. It lives in the menu bar, not the Dock.

Install

  1. Download the zip above and open it, if your browser didn't already. Drag Snapkeep into your Applications folder.
  2. Open Snapkeep. Because it isn't notarized by Apple, macOS says it can't check the app. Click Done, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about Snapkeep and click Open Anyway.
  3. When Snapkeep asks for Screen Recording permission, turn it on in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording. Then quit Snapkeep from its menu and open it again.

A camera icon appears in the menu bar. To start Snapkeep with your Mac, choose Open at Login in its menu.

Prefer the Terminal? Instead of step 2, run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Snapkeep.app.
Updating? Replace the app in Applications with the new one. macOS asks for Screen Recording permission again after each update. Your version is shown at the bottom of the Snapkeep menu.

Shortcuts

ShortcutAction
⇧⌘1Capture an area or a window
⇧⌘2Capture the text in an area and copy it
⇧⌘7Record an area or a window. Press it again to stop.
